# 🌍 About DTC Wonders

**DinoTechnology Center (DTC) - Wonders** is a Tanzanian technology studio building **voice AI for African languages**, starting with **Swahili**.

Most AI voices sound translated, with the wrong tone, rhythm and accent. We are building voice tools that sound right to the people who use them: shop owners, creators, radio stations, schools and teams across East Africa.

We also build custom software for businesses and organizations, which is where we started and what keeps us shipping.

---

# 🎯 Mission & Vision

**Mission:** To make every Swahili-speaking business and creator sound professional, local and trusted, by making natural Swahili voice affordable and easy to use.

**Vision:** A future where Swahili and other African languages are first-class in voice technology, so no business or creator has to sound translated to be heard.

| Area | Description |

---

# 🔬 Research

We focus on narrow, local questions that global models often miss:

- How natural and trustworthy do Swahili voices sound to Tanzanian listeners?
- How do regional accents and everyday Swahili differ from the standard voices in global tools?
- What makes a Swahili ad or announcement sound right for radio and social media?

**Our principles**
- **Consent first:** voice recordings are collected only with clear permission, and voice contributors are fairly paid.
- **No voice cloning without written consent.**
- **Honest claims:** we publish what we tested and how, and we say what isn't ready yet.
